Our Story

A Piece of France in Downtown Phnom Penh

Since 2011, Bouchon Wine Bar has been serving authentic French cuisine and an extensive selection of wines imported directly from France, in a charming colonial house on Street 174.

Choose the cozy indoor dining room, the wine bar for a glass and a chat, or our outdoor terrace under the frangipani trees — with live Khmer and French music every Wednesday and Friday night.

Signature dishes include Beef Carpaccio, Boeuf Bourguignon, Foie Gras and hand-cut Beef Tartare, paired with a wine list built by our sommelier.
